# Sage OS mission

## Mission

**An AI that knows your work, on your own computer.** Sage OS is an app anyone can download: install it, give it
your documents, and talk to an AI that answers from them, keeps what it learns as notes you can read, and does
useful work on its own, like a brief before each meeting or a summary every morning. It never changes anything
without asking, and your data never leaves your devices.

## The problem

- People hear "AI agents" everywhere, but for most of them it's a buzzword: something for developers with
  terminals, API keys and config files. What would an agent even do for them?
- The AI they do use starts from zero every time. Their documents, notes and past conversations are scattered, so
  every answer is generic.
- Handing work to an AI feels risky: what did it change, and can it be undone?

## What Sage OS is

| Pillar | What it does for a person |
|---|---|
| **Installs like any app** | One download for Windows or Mac, no terminal, no account. It updates itself. |
| **Any AI, including a free one** | A model that runs on the computer itself (private, free, offline), or ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude if they already use one. |
| **Knows your work** | Your documents go on the Bookshelf; answers come from them and show where each fact came from. |
| **Helpers that do things** | Ready-made helpers you switch on, like a morning brief, a weekly recap, or meeting prep, that work on a schedule and bring results to the Inbox. This is what "an AI agent" means here, in practice. |
| **You stay in charge** | Nothing is saved or changed without your OK in the Inbox; everything can be undone; every helper's work is logged. |
| **Your devices, no cloud** | Your computers pair with each other directly; nothing is stored anywhere else (D43). |

## Who it's for

Someone who has used ChatGPT, maybe, and nothing else: no terminal, no "agents", no markdown (D41). They have
documents in many places and a busy calendar. Sage OS has to make sense to them in the first five minutes, on an
ordinary laptop (8 GB of memory, no graphics card).

## Principles

1. **Independent.** Sage OS stands alone: no Hermes, Claude Code, GPU or developer tool is needed for anything a
   person sees first. Integrations are extras.
2. **Five minutes to value.** From download to a useful answer about their own documents in about five minutes;
   every screen has one obvious next step (D41).
3. **Agents you can see.** A helper says in plain words what it will do, when, and what it did; its results wait for
   your OK.
4. **Local first, no outside services.** Data, models and sync stay on the person's own devices (D43). The only
   network calls are to the AIs they choose and to download the app, its updates and models.
5. **The human approves what matters.** Helpers propose; people decide. Undo is exact.
6. **Plain files you can walk away with.** Notes are markdown on disk, readable without Sage OS.
7. **Honest status, calm screens.** Show what is actually running or stuck; ask for attention only when needed.

## Why "Sage"

1. **A wise mentor:** it plans, reviews and teaches, not only answers.
2. **The colour green:** sage is a green; the phosphor theme, agent orbs and the S-trail icon follow it.
3. **SAGE: Semi-Autonomous Agent Ground Environment:** a nod to SAGE (Semi-Automatic Ground Environment), the 1950s
   air-defence network run from rooms of CRT consoles.

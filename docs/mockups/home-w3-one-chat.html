What's the difference between present value and future value? Keep it short.
Local · Gemma 4 12B
Present value is what money arriving later is worth today, found by discounting it at a rate. Future value runs the other way: what money you have now grows to by a later date. Same formula, opposite direction: PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ.

## The five-minute demo (slide 4)

Run it on an ordinary laptop, not the founder's GPU machine.

1. **Install:** open the download, start the app. It's empty and says what to do.
2. **Pick an AI:** the starter model downloads and starts (or paste a free Gemini key).
3. **Add documents:** drag in three real documents (a deal memo, meeting notes, a report).
4. **Ask:** "What did we agree with Acme last time?" The answer cites the meeting notes; click through to the source.
5. **Helpers:** switch on *Meeting prep*; show the brief it wrote for tomorrow's meeting, waiting in the Inbox; approve
   it; show it's undoable.
6. **Trust:** the activity log shows every step the helper took; nothing left the computer except the question to
   the AI you picked.

## Questions to be ready for

- *Why won't ChatGPT or Google just do this?* They're cloud-first and want your data in their cloud; we're
  local-first, any-AI, and built around approval. Some people will always want the second.
- *How do you get users without a cloud account to track them?* Downloads, update checks and opt-in feedback; see
  business-model.md for the paid tier that funds it.
- *What stops a big local-AI app from adding this?* The Bookshelf with approval and undo, helpers that act on a
  schedule, and device-to-device sync are a product, not a feature; see technology.md.

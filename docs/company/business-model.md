# Business model (not decided)

No commercial model is chosen yet (MISSION.md, *Company framing*). These are the options that fit the product's
principles: local first, no outside services, the person owns their data. Pick one to test first and record it as a
decision.

| Option | How it works | Fits because | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Free app, paid Pro** | The app, local models and the Bookshelf are free; Pro adds helpers beyond a few, meeting prep, the note-taker, more devices | People try it with no friction; the paid part is what saves them time | Choosing the line so free is useful and Pro is obviously worth it |
| **B. Team plan** | Shared, access-controlled bookshelves for a firm (O1), run on the firm's own machine; per-seat licence | The first demo asked for it; firms pay for control over client data | Needs O1 and admin features first |
| **C. One-time licence + paid upgrades** | Buy once, own it; major versions paid | Matches "your computer, your data"; no subscription fatigue | Less predictable revenue |
| **D. Bundled AI credits** | Optional cloud AI access billed through Sage OS for people without their own key | Removes the API-key step for newcomers | Becomes a reseller of other companies' AI; margin and trust questions; conflicts with "no outside services" unless clearly optional |

**Licence checks without a server.** A signed licence file that the app verifies offline (the same way it verifies
updates) keeps D43's no-outside-services promise.

**What to measure first.** TO FILL with the chosen option: conversion from free to paid, retention after 30 days,
helpers switched on per user. Measured how? Opt-in feedback and the user's own consent; no hidden analytics.

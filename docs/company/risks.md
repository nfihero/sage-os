# Risks and what we do about them

| Risk | Why it matters | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Big AI companies add memory, projects and agents** | They have the best models and the users | Stay where they won't go: local-first, any AI, data on the person's devices, approval and undo. Work with their models rather than against them. |
| **Local models aren't good enough on ordinary laptops** | The free path is the first impression | Pick the best model that fits each machine; make cloud AIs one click away; measure answer quality on real documents (the K14 evaluation method). |
| **Install friction (OS warnings, big downloads)** | People quit before the first answer | Signed and notarized installers (U1); a starter model of a few GB; time-to-first-answer as a release gate (U2). |
| **"Agents" stay abstract** | Without a clear use, people don't come back | Ready-made helpers with plain descriptions and visible results (U3); the meeting-prep and recap use cases from real demos. |
| **Trust: an AI changing someone's files** | One bad change loses a user for good | Nothing changes without approval; exact undo; secret screening; trust levels earned over time; a full log. |
| **Security of device-to-device sync** | A listener on the network is an attack surface | Runs only when paired or pairing; TLS; signed requests bound to the connection; one-time codes; tested refusals (D45). |
| **No usage data (no analytics by design)** | Hard to learn what works | Opt-in feedback, Report a problem, tester interviews; never hidden tracking. |
| **One-person team** | Speed and bus factor | TO FILL: hiring plan; meanwhile, every decision and release is documented so others can pick it up (DECISIONS, HANDOFF). |
| **Third-party AI costs and terms** | Prices change; providers change rules | Show real costs per AI, a dated price list the user can correct, and a free local path that needs no provider. |

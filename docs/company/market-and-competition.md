# Market and competition

## Who buys first

**Beachhead:** professionals whose work is documents and meetings, who already use one or two AIs, and who are careful
about where client or company data goes: consultants, finance and deal teams, lawyers, researchers, founders.
The first outside demo (an investment professional, 14 Oct 2026) asked for exactly this: briefs before meetings, a
note-taker, recurring jobs, and a library a team can share with access control.

**Then:** small teams in the same fields (shared, access-controlled bookshelves, O1), and anyone who wants a private AI
that knows their documents.

## The alternatives people use today

| They use | Good at | Where Sage OS differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloud chat apps with memory and projects** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) | The best models; easy | Their memory and projects live in one vendor's cloud and work with that vendor's AI only. Sage OS keeps data on the person's devices and works with all of them. |
| **Notebook-style research tools** (e.g. NotebookLM) | Answers grounded in uploaded sources | Cloud-hosted, one vendor's model, and they answer rather than act. Sage OS adds helpers that work on a schedule, with approval and undo. |
| **Local-model runners** (e.g. LM Studio, Ollama apps, Jan, AnythingLLM) | Running models privately | Built for people who already know what a model is. Sage OS picks one that fits the machine and wraps it in a product: Bookshelf, Inbox, helpers. |
| **Note apps with AI** (e.g. Notion AI, Obsidian with plugins) | Writing and organizing notes | AI is an add-on to the notes. In Sage OS the AI does the work and the person approves it; notes stay plain files. |
| **Meeting assistants** (cloud note-takers) | Transcripts and summaries | Recordings go to their cloud. Sage OS's note-taker (M2) transcribes on the computer and files notes under the people involved. |

Check each competitor's current features before a meeting; this table states positioning, not a feature audit.

## Where we win

Local-first and any-AI, with helpers that do real work and an approval inbox that makes it safe to let them. That
combination is the corner none of the alternatives occupy.
